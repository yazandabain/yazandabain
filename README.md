- evaluated resource-targeting strategies under controlled graph interventions
- separated preregistered analysis from post-result exploratory controls
- produced a reproducible research paper with automated numerical claim verification

**Python · Network Analysis · Research Engineering · Reproducible Research · AI Safety**

### [Budapest Transit Research Pipeline](https://github.com/yazandabain/bkk-collector)

24/7 data pipeline collecting Budapest GTFS-Realtime transit data for later delay prediction and ML research.

- fault-tolerant realtime collection at high frequency
- crash-safe Parquet storage and recovery
- data-quality manifests and integrity checks
- verified off-site backups and retention tooling
- Dockerized deployment on a Linux VPS

**Python · Data Engineering · GTFS-Realtime · Parquet · Docker · Reliability Engineering**
